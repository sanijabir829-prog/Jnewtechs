JAB-SAN TECH is a technology intelligence dashboard developed to demonstrate the application of data science, data analysis and visualization in understanding the changing technology landscape.

Project Objectives

1. Explore emerging technology trends.


2. Examine artificial intelligence applications.


3. Understand programming and digital skills.


4. Highlight technology career opportunities.


5. Communicate insights through data visualization.
